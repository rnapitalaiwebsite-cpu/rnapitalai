What an engineer does each day

The app runs on the engineer’s own phone. Try the Clock out button.

  • Clock in on arrival. GPS confirms they are at the project site.
  • Log the day’s work in a line or two, with photos from the phone camera.
  • Flag an issue straight to operations, with a photo.
  • Request leave and submit the weekly timesheet, which feeds payroll.
  • Works with weak signal. Entries wait on the phone and send when signal returns.
